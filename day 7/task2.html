Steps To Make Chocolate Cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease and line a round baking tin with parchment paper.
  2. In a bowl, mix together flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and a pinch of salt. Sift the dry ingredients for a smooth texture.
  3. In another bowl, whisk sugar and eggs until light and fluffy. Add oil, milk, and vanilla extract. Mix well.
  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture. Stir slowly until the batter is smooth and lump-free.
  5. Add warm water or coffee to enhance flavor and get a soft texture. Mix gently.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared tin and tap lightly to remove air bubbles.
  7. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  8. Once baked, remove from oven and let it cool for 10 minutes before taking it out of the tin.
  9. For frosting, mix butter, cocoa powder, icing sugar, and milk until creamy and smooth.
  10. Spread the chocolate frosting over the cooled cake and decorate with chocolate chips or grated chocolate.
