Comparing Different Margin Values

This box has a smaller margin. It stays closer to the surrounding elements, showing how tighter spacing looks on a webpage.

This box has a larger margin. It appears farther away from other elements, giving more white space and a cleaner layout.

Smaller margins are useful when you want elements to appear grouped together, like in cards or list items.

Larger margins are often used to separate sections, such as between a header and the main content area.
